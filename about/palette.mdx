# Palette

Select Solarized light, Solarized dark or Omicron from the Palette toolbar.
The OpenSCD slots are listed first, followed by the 27 MD3 roles oscd-ui uses.
[The mapping table](https://github.com/OMICRONEnergyOSS/oscd-ui/blob/main/THEMING.md#material-role-mapping)
shows which slot supplies each MD3 role and its fallback.
